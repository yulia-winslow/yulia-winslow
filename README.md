### Yulia Winslow

**Building [Broken Bot](https://brokenbot.tech)**, a 15-minute live reaction game for two.

Unexpected word pairs come one after another, each player reacts on the spot, and at the end both get statistics on how they think on their feet.

- **Investor and founder:** an angel investor plays one round with a founder, and both get the statistics.
- **Co-founders:** two founders play together and get one report on how they work as a pair.

Each reaction is read through a classification I developed, so the statistics show not only how fast someone reacts but what kind of thinking the reaction carries.

**Why.** Investors and co-founders bet most on how a person handles the unexpected, and have no data on it. Broken Bot collects that data: how two people move through a fast-changing environment together, turn by turn. Nobody collects this yet.

**Where it's going.** From pairs to teams: how a whole team moves through the unexpected together.
